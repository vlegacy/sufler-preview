Only iPhone.

There is no iPad, Mac or Android version. The interface comes in 11 languages, Ukrainian, English and Russian among them, and follows the iPhone's language.

No. Nothing leaves the iPhone.

Speech is recognised on the iPhone itself, and nothing leaves it. Follow my voice comes with Pro and is for scripts in English, Ukrainian or Russian. It asks for the microphone and speech recognition and needs the iPhone's offline speech model for that language. When it cannot hear you, the line keeps the pace you set.

With Pro, in a window.

A Picture in Picture window. The default window, Over the camera, is made to stay over camera apps but has no buttons; iOS dims the window with buttons while a camera is on. Some camera apps show no such window at all, so for video, record inside MySufler.

Under its script, then Photos.

Each take is listed under the script it was shot from, then saved to Photos if you allow it, or to Files if not. If the microphone is not allowed, the video is silent.
