MySufler: Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026. Developer: Viktor Skrynnikov. For MySufler (formerly Sufler) on iPhone, and on Android once it is on Google Play.

In short: the MySufler app collects no data about you. Your scripts, videos and settings stay on your phone. The app sends them neither to us nor to anyone else.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • The scripts you type, paste or import from a .txt, .rtf, .docx or .pdf file you pick, and the folders you sort them into, in the app's own folder.
  • Settings: pace, the size and look of the text, the position of the line and the control pad.
  • The free reading counter: how many seconds of the free time are used. It is kept in the phone's keychain and sent nowhere.
  • The videos you record: each one is saved to your Photos library and filed under the script it was shot from. For each take the app keeps a small picture of it and, while there is room, a copy to play, in its own folder. If Photos refuses access, the video stays in the app's folder, which is visible in the Files app.

Permissions the iPhone app asks for

  • Camera: to show the picture and record video.
  • Microphone: to record sound in the video, and to follow your voice (MySufler Pro). Without it, video is recorded silent.
  • Photos, add only: to save the recorded video. The app does not read your library.
  • Speech recognition: only for “Follow my voice” (MySufler Pro). Speech is recognised on the iPhone itself; no audio or text leaves it.

You can withdraw any of them at any time in Settings → MySufler.

Purchases on iPhone

MySufler Pro is bought through the App Store on iPhone. Apple handles the payment: we do not receive your name, email address or card details. The app learns one thing from the system: whether Pro is active.

What the app does not have

The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party data-collection libraries. We run no servers: on iPhone the app uses the internet only through the App Store, to show the prices and to check a purchase.

On Android (Google Play)

The Android app is not on Google Play yet. It works the same way and collects nothing either; this section applies from its first release.

  • Stored on the phone, in the app's own storage: your scripts and folders (typed, pasted or imported from .txt, .rtf, .docx or .pdf files you pick), the settings, and the free reading counter. Android's own backup to your Google account (if you have it on) may include them; it goes to your account, not to us.
  • Recorded videos are saved to your gallery, in the Movies/Sufler folder. The app lists your own takes; it does not read the rest of your gallery.
  • Permissions: Camera (picture and video), Microphone (sound in the video, and following your voice), Notifications (the pause and speed buttons while the floating window is on), Display over other apps (the floating text window, MySufler Pro). Speech is recognised on the phone itself (Android 12 and later); no audio or text leaves the phone. If the phone has no on-device recognition, the text keeps its own pace. Each permission can be withdrawn in Android Settings → Apps → MySufler.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. Google handles the payment: we do not receive your name, email address or card details. The app learns from Google Play only whether Pro is active. The app's only internet use is Google Play Billing, to show prices and check a purchase.
  • Deleting the app deletes its scripts, settings and counter; recorded videos stay in your gallery until you delete them.

This website

The website mysufler.app (the landing page and these pages) is separate from the app. It has no accounts or forms. It uses two analytics tools, and only after you accept them on the cookie card: Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited) and Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation). Until you choose, neither is loaded and no request goes to Google or Microsoft.

  • What they collect: the pages you open, clicks, how far you scroll, the type of device and browser, and your approximate country. Clarity also records sessions and heatmaps, with all the text of the page masked. Google and Microsoft each receive your IP address with every request to their servers; for visitors from the EU, Switzerland and the UK, Google Analytics 4 does not log or store it.
  • How long, and what for: Google Analytics keeps event data for 2 months; Clarity keeps recordings for 30 days and heatmaps for 9 months. Google signals and advertising features are off. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for ads.
  • Cookies: _ga and _ga_<id> (Google Analytics, 13 months), _clck (Clarity, 1 year), _clsk (Clarity, 1 day). Your choice is kept in your browser's local storage (mysufler-consent) for 12 months; then the card asks again.
  • App Store links: these do not depend on the card. Each carries a campaign tag with the page language and the place of the link on the page (the QR code has its own tag). Apple shows us only totals per tag in App Store Connect, and only once at least 5 Apple Accounts have installed the app. The tag holds nothing about you.
  • To refuse or withdraw: press Reject on the card, or open “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page and press Reject: those cookies are deleted. You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser's settings. The site works the same either way.

Children

The app collects data from no one, children included. The website is covered in the section above.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and its scripts and settings are gone. On iPhone, recorded videos stay in Photos until you delete them yourself; videos left in the app's folder are deleted with the app. A backup of your phone made by iOS (iCloud or a computer) includes the app's scripts, settings and the free reading counter. The free reading counter stays in the phone's keychain after the app is deleted, so that the free time does not start again; it holds nothing but a number of seconds. On Android, see the section above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
